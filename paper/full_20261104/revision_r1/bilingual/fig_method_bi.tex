\caption{One training step, drawn on an instance with a depot (square) and four targets (dots). Top row: the frozen reference policy $p_{\mathrm{ref}}$ samples orders and one is chosen as the anchor $q$; solving it fixes the take-off and landing points of every sortie (blue); local search reconnects the same sorties (dashed) and reads each cost from the table $C^{q}$; the best candidate $\hat{\pi}$ is solved and becomes the expert $\pi^{E}$. Bottom row: the routes of the learning policy $p_{\theta}$ are scored on the same sorties, which bounds their optimal cost from above (Proposition~\ref{prop:bound}); only the route with the lowest bound is solved (orange bar), and the policy is updated by REINFORCE and by imitation of the expert. Double arrows are the exact SOCP solves, 2.89 per training instance on average up to E133. Table~\ref{alg:train} gives the full step.\protect\newline\zhcap{一个训练步骤，画在一个含基地（方块）和四个目标（圆点）的算例上。上行：冻结的参考策略 $p_{\mathrm{ref}}$ 采样若干顺序，其中一个被选为锚点 $q$；求解它就确定了每个架次的起飞点和降落点（蓝色）；局部搜索保留这些架次、只改变它们的连接方式（虚线），并从表 $C^{q}$ 中读出每个代价；最佳候选 $\hat{\pi}$ 经求解后成为专家 $\pi^{E}$。下行：学习策略 $p_{\theta}$ 的路线在同一组架次上打分，得到其最优代价的上界（命题~\ref{prop:bound}）；只有上界最低的路线被求解（橙色柱），策略通过 REINFORCE 和对专家的模仿进行更新。双线箭头是精确 SOCP 求解，截至 E133 平均每个训练实例 2.89 次。表~\ref{alg:train} 给出完整步骤。}}
