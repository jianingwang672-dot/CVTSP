Abstract
In the carrier–vehicle traveling salesman problem (CVTSP), a carrier launches
and recovers a drone at continuous points while the drone visits a set of
targets. Evaluating one visiting order requires solving a second-order cone
program (SOCP), so training a deep reinforcement learning (DRL) policy
on exact rewards for every sampled route consumes hundreds of millions of
solver calls. We show that solving one order already gives a feasible solution
for every other order: keep the take-off and landing points of each target
and let the carrier drive between them in the new order. The cost of this
solution, read from a table without a solver, is therefore an upper bound on
the optimal cost of any order, and it is exact for the order that was solved.
We build a training recipe around this bound: it gives a reward to every sam-
pled route, guides solver-free local search and ranks candidate expert routes,
while exact solves are reserved for a few routes per instance and for every
imitation label. With the same 3.84 million exact solves, POMO trained on
exact rewards is on average 3.8% worse per instance than our policy on 529
literature benchmark instances. A POMO baseline trained with 299 million
solves, 78 times as many as ours in training, remains better than our pol-
icy, by 0.21% on the same instances and 1.13% on synthetic test instances.
At equal computing time, iterated local search and variable neighbourhood
search are worse than our policy on these 529 instances, but with ten times
the time they find better solutions than both learned policies. The recipe is
therefore a way to obtain a learned CVTSP solver with a small solver budget,
not a better solver.

1. Introduction 引言
    A drone with a short flight range can serve targets far beyond that range
if a long-range platform carries it. A ship can launch an unmanned aerial
vehicle (UAV) towards an offshore installation, keep moving, and recover
the UAV further along its course. The carrier–vehicle traveling salesman
problem (CVTSP) (Garone et al., 2011) models this operation. A carrier
leaves a depot, releases a drone before each target, recovers it after the visit
and returns to the depot once all targets have been served. Each release,
visit and recovery is a sortie, and the carrier’s travel from the landing point
of one sortie to the take-off point of the next is a carrier leg. The planner
chooses the visiting order and the take-off and landing point of each sortie
in order to minimise mission completion time.
  航程较短的无人机若由远程平台携带，就能服务远超其航程的目标。船舶可以
向海上设施发射一架无人机（UAV），继续航行，并在航线更远处回收它。载体—
无人机旅行商问题（CVTSP）(Garone et al., 2011) 刻画的就是这种作业。载体从
基地出发，在每个目标之前释放无人机，在访问之后回收它，并在服务完所有目标
后返回基地。每一次释放、访问和回收称为一个架次；载体从一个架次的降落点行
驶到下一个架次起飞点的这段路程称为一个载体航段。规划者需要选择访问顺序
以及每个架次的起飞点和降落点，使任务完成时间最短。

    The problem combines two kinds of decisions: the visiting order, which
is a permutation, and the take-off and landing points of the drone, which lie
in the continuous plane. Once the order is fixed, the remaining problem is
convex and can be written as an SOCP (Lobo et al., 1998); when the order is
also a decision, the problem becomes a mixed-integer SOCP. Exact methods
solve it only for small instances (Gambella et al., 2018; Erdoğan and Yıldırım,
2021); the largest instances proved optimal so far have 25 targets, plus one
with 35 (Vieira et al., 2026). Larger instances are solved by heuristics, such
as the two-stage iterative heuristic of Li et al. (2026), which compute every
instance from scratch.
   该问题结合了两类决策：访问顺序是一个排列，而无人机的起飞点和降落点位
于连续平面上。顺序固定后，剩余问题是凸的，可写成 SOCP (Lobo et al., 1998)；
当顺序也是决策变量时，问题成为混合整数 SOCP。精确方法只能求解小规模实
例 (Gambella et al., 2018; Erdoğan and Yıldırım, 2021)；目前被证明最优的最大
实例有 25 个目标，另有一个 35 个目标的实例 (Vieira et al., 2026)。更大的实例
依靠启发式求解，例如 Li et al. (2026) 的两阶段迭代启发式，它们对每个实例都
从头计算。
    Deep reinforcement learning has become a practical alternative for routing
problems when solutions must be produced quickly. A trained policy pro-
duces a route in a single forward pass, and methods such as POMO (Kwon
et al., 2020) reach small gaps on the traveling salesman problem. These
methods are trained by sampling millions of routes and scoring each one,
which is cheap when the cost of a route is a sum of known edge lengths. In
the CVTSP it is not: the carrier legs depend on where the drone takes off and
lands, and these points are optimised jointly for the whole route, so scoring
one route means solving an SOCP. Our reference baseline, POMO trained
on an exact solver reward for every sampled route, made about 3 × 108 solver
calls over 200 epochs. Within the range of speeds and endurance seen in
training, such a policy can be reused without retraining, because these pa-
rameters enter the network as inputs. A new variant of the problem, such as
one with additional constraints or a different spatial distribution of targets,
may require training from scratch. The solver budget of that training then
decides whether building a learned solver is worthwhile at all.
  当需要快速给出解时，深度强化学习已成为路径问题的一种实用替代方法。训
练好的策略一次前向计算即可生成路线，POMO (Kwon et al., 2020) 等方法在旅
行商问题上已达到很小的差距。这些方法在训练中要采样数百万条路线并逐条打
分；当路线代价是已知边长之和时，这一步代价很低。CVTSP 不是这样：载体航
段取决于无人机在哪里起飞和降落，而这些点是针对整条路线联合优化的，因此

    This paper asks whether most of those calls are necessary. The answer
rests on one structural observation. In the fixed-order SOCP, the constraints
on a sortie concern one target only, and the visiting order enters only through
the carrier legs between sorties. If the sortie points from one optimal solution
are kept and the order is changed, every sortie remains feasible and only the
carrier legs change. The resulting value is an upper bound on the optimal cost
of the new order, and it can be computed by adding up entries of a table. One
exact solve thus says something about every route of the instance, including
the routes that will never be solved.
  本文要回答的问题是：这些调用中的大多数是否必要。答案建立在一个结构性
观察之上。在定序 SOCP 中，每个架次的约束只涉及一个目标，访问顺序只通过
架次之间的载体航段进入问题。如果保留一个最优解中的架次点而改变顺序，每
个架次仍然可行，只有载体航段发生变化。由此得到的值是新顺序最优代价的一
个上界，并且可以通过对一张表中的元素求和得到。因此，一次精确求解就能提供
该实例中每条路线的信息，包括那些永远不会被求解的路线。
    Erdoğan and Yıldırım (2021) already use the idea of keeping sortie points
fixed. In their iterated local search, a neighbouring order keeps the take-
off and landing points of the current solution, so its cost can be computed
without a new solve. What we have not found is the use of this idea to
train a DRL policy. Self-improvement methods, such as the multi-action
method of Luttmann and Xie (2026), compute the exact cost of every sampled
solution, which is cheap in the problems they study. In our recipe, the bound
replaces this computation. It gives the reward for routes that are never
solved, and it ranks candidate labels, each of which is solved exactly before
it is used. Section 2.4 compares our method with the closest CVTSP and
learning methods.

2. Related work 相关工作
  Three strands of work bear on this paper: solution methods for the
CVTSP, DRL for routing, and learning under expensive objectives.
  与本文相关的工作有三条脉络：CVTSP 的求解方法、用于路径问题的 DRL，
以及昂贵目标下的学习。

2.1. The CVTSP and its extensions CVTSP 及其扩展
    Garone et al. (2011) studied the problem and proposed a two-step ap-
proach that first fixes an order and then optimises the sortie points. Garone

                                     6
et al. (2014) studied the variant in which the vehicle visits several targets per
sortie. Gambella et al. (2018) gave a mixed-integer SOCP formulation and
an exact algorithm that solves instances with up to 15 targets; the algorithm
ranks visiting orders by a lower bound and solves fixed-order problems in that
order, which is an exact-method precedent for screening orders with a cheap
bound. Erdoğan and Yıldırım (2021) proposed a formulation with stronger
lower bounds and an iterated local search whose neighbourhood moves keep
the take-off and landing points of each target, so that a neighbouring or-
der receives a feasible value without a solve. Poikonen and Golden (2020)
studied the mothership and drone routing problem, with instances of up to
200 targets; Erdoğan and Yıldırım (2021) describe it as essentially the same
problem as the CVTSP. Fahradyan et al. (2020) extended the CVTSP to two
carriers that share one vehicle, and Li et al. (2024) studied a joint routing
problem with several vessels and UAVs, solved with a tabu search heuristic.
   Garone et al. (2011) 研究了该问题，并提出先固定顺序、再优化架次点的
两步法。Garone et al. (2014) 研究了无人机在一个架次中访问多个目标的变体。
Gambella et al. (2018) 给出了一个混合整数 SOCP 模型和一个精确算法，可求解
最多 15 个目标的实例；该算法按下界给访问顺序排序，并依次求解定序问题，这
是精确方法中用廉价下界筛选顺序的先例。Erdoğan and Yıldırım (2021) 提出了下
界更强的模型，以及一种迭代局部搜索，其邻域移动保留每个目标的起飞点和降落
点，从而无需求解即可为相邻顺序给出一个可行值。Poikonen and Golden (2020)
研究了母舰—无人机路径问题，其实例最多有 200 个目标；Erdoğan and Yıldırım
(2021) 认为它与 CVTSP 本质上是同一问题。Fahradyan et al. (2020) 把 CVTSP
扩展到两个载体共用一架无人机的情形，Li et al. (2024) 研究了多艘船舶与多架
无人机的联合路径问题，并用禁忌搜索启发式求解。
    Recent work has moved the exact and heuristic frontiers. Li et al. (2026)
developed exact methods and a two-stage iterative heuristic. Vieira et al.
(2026) propose a branch-and-cut algorithm that iteratively corrects the error
of a linear approximation; it solves all benchmark instances with up to 25
customers to optimality, and one instance with 35 customers. Ren et al.
(2027) address a vessel with multiple UAVs through a mixed-integer SOCP
and a parallel adaptive large neighbourhood search. CVTSP research is one
branch of a wider literature on drone-assisted routing (Murray and Chu,
2015; Agatz et al., 2018; Otto et al., 2018; Macrina et al., 2020; Chung et al.,
2020). All of the CVTSP methods above solve each instance from scratch
and spend their computing time per instance. A trained policy moves most
of that time into training, although at test time it still solves one SOCP per
distinct route it proposes.

从头求解，把计算时间花在每个实例上。训练好的策略把大部分时间移到训练阶
段，不过在测试时，它仍要为提出的每条不同路线求解一个 SOCP。
    Two observations from this literature shape our design. The first is that
the fixed-order problem is the unit of exact evaluation: the exact cost of a
candidate order requires one SOCP. The stronger heuristics avoid paying it
for every candidate, either by keeping sortie points fixed during local search
(Erdoğan and Yıldırım, 2021) or by searching on an approximate matrix
(Li et al., 2026). The second is that the order and the sortie points are
strongly coupled, since a good order depends on where the carrier will be,
and the carrier’s position depends on the order. Two-stage methods handle
this coupling by alternating between the two levels. Our table can be read
as one such alternation step, frozen and reused across a whole search.
    这些文献中的两项观察塑造了我们的设计。第一，定序问题是精确评估的基
本单位：一个候选顺序的精确代价需要一次 SOCP 求解。较强的启发式方法会避
免为每个候选都付出这一代价，要么在局部搜索中保持架次点不变 (Erdoğan and
Yıldırım, 2021)，要么在近似矩阵上搜索 (Li et al., 2026)。第二，顺序和架次点强
耦合：好的顺序取决于载体将处于何处，而载体的位置又取决于顺序。两阶段方法
通过在两个层级之间交替来处理这种耦合。我们的表可以看作一次这样的交替步
骤，被冻结下来并在整个搜索中复用。

2.2. Deep reinforcement learning for routing 用于路径问题的深度强化学
     习
    Most deep reinforcement learning methods for routing are developed for
the travelling salesman problem (TSP) and the capacitated vehicle routing
problem, in which trucks with limited load serve customers. In both prob-
lems, customers are fixed points and a route is an ordering of these points.
Learning to construct routes began with pointer networks (Vinyals et al.,
2015) and was extended to vehicle routing by Nazari et al. (2018). The
attention model of Kool et al. (2019) established the encoder–decoder archi-
tecture that most later work adopts. POMO (Kwon et al., 2020) exploits
the multiple symmetric starts of a route, and Sym-NCO (Kim et al., 2022)

                                      8
and eﬀicient active search (Hottung et al., 2022) add symmetry training and
instance-level adaptation. Improvement-based policies (Wu et al., 2022) and
heavy-decoder models (Luo et al., 2023) target better quality and larger sizes.
Surveys by Bengio et al. (2021) and Mazyavkina et al. (2021) cover the field,
and Wu et al. (2026) apply it to clustered vehicle routing in this journal. In
these problems, the cost of a route is the sum of its edge lengths, and the
load limit is checked by simple comparisons. A reward therefore costs one
pass over the route, and no optimisation problem has to be solved.
    路径问题的深度强化学习方法大多针对旅行商问题（TSP）和带容量约束的车
辆路径问题（即载重有限的卡车服务客户）。两类问题中客户都是固定的点，一条
路线就是这些点的一个排序。学习构造路线始于指针网络 (Vinyals et al., 2015)，
Nazari et al. (2018) 将其扩展到车辆路径问题。Kool et al. (2019) 的注意力模型确
立了此后大多数工作采用的编码器—解码器结构。POMO (Kwon et al., 2020) 利
用路线的多个对称起点，Sym-NCO (Kim et al., 2022) 和高效主动搜索 (Hottung
et al., 2022) 加入了对称性训练和实例级自适应。基于改进的策略 (Wu et al., 2022)
和重解码器模型 (Luo et al., 2023) 追求更好的质量和更大的规模。Bengio et al.
(2021) 和 Mazyavkina et al. (2021) 的综述覆盖了这一领域，Wu et al. (2026) 在
本刊将其应用于聚类车辆路径问题。在这些问题中，一条路线的成本就是各段边
长之和，载重限制只需简单比较即可检查。因此计算一次奖励只需遍历一遍路线，
不需要求解任何优化问题。
    The CVTSP combines two decisions: a visiting order, as in the TSP,
and, for that order, the continuous points where the drone leaves and rejoins
the carrier for each target. A visiting order alone therefore does not fix
the cost of a route; the second decision requires solving an SOCP. DRL
methods for truck–drone routing (Bogyrbayeva et al., 2023; Peng et al., 2025)
restrict launch and landing to network nodes, so their reward is still computed
directly. Fewer learning methods address problems with continuous launch
and landing points. Two studies learn policies for mothership problems, in
which drones are launched from and recovered by a moving carrier. Yan
et al. (2026) study a truck that serves as a moving launch and recovery
platform for two drones delivering to moving targets, and train a multi-agent
proximal policy optimisation method. Liu et al. (2026) apply proximal policy
optimisation to eVTOL-and-drone routing with no-fly zones; the eVTOL
chooses among a finite set of moves on a grid, and the policy is retrained
for each instance. The single-drone mothership problem is the CVTSP itself
(Erdoğan and Yıldırım, 2021), so we do not claim that learning methods are
absent from this class of problems. In both studies, however, the reward
is computed by the simulated environment from elapsed time, distance and

                                      9
penalty terms, without solving an optimisation problem; the take-off and
landing points result from the policy’s step-by-step moves in the simulation.
In the CVTSP formulation used here, the policy chooses only the visiting
order, and the take-off and landing points are then optimised exactly for
that order by solving an SOCP, so the cost of an order is known only after
this solve. Our argument concerns the cost of training when every reward
requires such a solve.
    CVTSP 包含两层决策：一是访问顺序，与 TSP 相同；二是在给定顺序下，
无人机对每个目标离开和返回载体的连续位置。因此仅凭访问顺序无法确定路
线成本，第二层决策需要求解一个 SOCP。卡车—无人机路径问题的 DRL 方法
(Bogyrbayeva et al., 2023; Peng et al., 2025) 把起飞和降落限制在网络节点上，因
此其奖励仍可直接计算。针对起降点连续的问题，学习方法较少。有两项研究为
母舰类问题学习策略，即无人机从移动载体起飞并由其回收。Yan et al. (2026) 研
究一辆卡车作为两架无人机的移动起降平台、向移动目标配送的问题，并训练一
种多智能体近端策略优化方法。Liu et al. (2026) 将近端策略优化用于带禁飞区的
eVTOL 与无人机路径问题；eVTOL 在网格上从有限的移动动作中选择，且策略
需针对每个实例重新训练。单无人机的母舰问题就是 CVTSP 本身 (Erdoğan and
Yıldırım, 2021)，因此我们不主张这类问题中没有学习方法。但在这两项研究中，
奖励都由仿真环境根据经过的时间、距离和惩罚项计算，不需要求解优化问题；起
飞点和降落点由策略在仿真中逐步的移动动作产生。而在本文采用的 CVTSP 形
式中，策略只选择访问顺序，起降点随后通过求解一个 SOCP 针对该顺序精确优
化，因此一个顺序的成本只有在这次求解之后才能知道。我们的论点关注的是每
个奖励都需要这样一次求解时的训练成本。

2.3. Expensive objectives and self-improvement 昂贵目标与自我改进
    In some combinatorial problems, evaluating the objective of a single so-
lution is itself costly, because it requires a simulation, an experiment or an
optimisation solve; in the CVTSP it requires an SOCP. Learning methods for
such problems either reduce the number of evaluations or replace evaluations
with a cheaper estimate. Kim et al. (2024) take the first approach: symmet-
ric replay training reuses solutions that have already been evaluated through
their symmetries, and thereby saves evaluations. It offers no estimate for
a route that was never evaluated. The second approach learns a surrogate.
Sun et al. (2026) train a neural network offline to estimate drone flight times
from trajectory-optimisation data and use it inside an order-first, split-second
heuristic for truck and drone delivery. SurCo (Ferber et al., 2023) learns lin-
ear surrogate costs for nonlinear combinatorial problems. These methods
learn or reuse a cost signal, and none of those we read provides a guarantee

                                      10
on routes outside the evaluated set. A learned surrogate can be accurate on
average, but it can also be wrong in a direction that is hard to detect, and
its training set has to be produced in advance. A bound derived from the
solution of the instance at hand needs no training data and has a known
direction of error, which makes it usable as a screening rule, although in our
runs it accepts relatively few routes on its own (Section 4.3).
  在一些组合优化问题中，计算单个解的目标值本身就很昂贵，因为它需要一次
仿真、实验或优化求解；在 CVTSP 中需要求解一个 SOCP。针对这类问题的学习
方法要么减少评估次数，要么用更便宜的估计代替评估。Kim et al. (2024) 采用第
一种思路：对称回放训练利用对称性重用已评估过的解，从而节省评估次数。它不
能对从未评估过的路线给出估计。第二种思路是学习代理模型。Sun et al. (2026)
用轨迹优化数据离线训练一个神经网络来估计无人机飞行时间，并在卡车—无人
机配送的“先排序、后拆分”启发式中使用它。SurCo (Ferber et al., 2023) 为非线
性组合问题学习线性代理代价。这些方法学习或复用代价信号，而在我们读过的
方法中，没有一个能对已评估集合之外的路线提供保证。学习得到的代理在平均
意义上可能准确，但也可能在难以察觉的方向上出错，并且其训练集必须事先生
成。由当前实例的解导出的上界无需训练数据，误差方向已知，因此可以用作筛选
规则，尽管在我们的实验中它单独接受的路线相对较少（第 4.3 节）。
    Self-improvement offers a second route to lower training cost, in which
a policy trains on labels that it produces itself. Luo et al. (2023) train by
supervised learning on optimal solutions of small instances; Luo et al. (2024)
remove the need for such labels by letting the model improve its own so-
lutions, and Pirnay and Grimm (2024) sample several solutions from the
current model and imitate the best one. Luttmann and Xie (2026) extend
the idea to problems in which several agents, such as vehicles or machines,
act together. On the problems these papers study, comparing two candidate
solutions is cheap, so labels can be accepted or rejected by direct evaluation.
In the CVTSP, the same test costs one SOCP per candidate. Our method
keeps the self-improvement loop and uses the bound to decide which candi-
dates deserve that cost. This changes the economics of the loop. Generating
a hundred candidates is cheap, but verifying a hundred candidates is not,
so the screening step becomes the part of the method that determines its
budget.
   自我改进是降低训练成本的另一条途径，即策略用自己产生的标签进行训练。
Luo et al. (2023) 在小规模实例的最优解上进行监督学习；Luo et al. (2024) 让模
型改进自己的解，从而不再需要这类标签；Pirnay and Grimm (2024) 从当前模型
采样多个解并模仿其中最好的一个。Luttmann and Xie (2026) 将这一思路扩展到
多个智能体（如多辆车或多台机器）协同行动的问题。在这些论文研究的问题上，


                                      11
比较两个候选解很廉价，因此可以直接评估来接受或拒绝标签。在 CVTSP 中，同
样的检验需要为每个候选求解一次 SOCP。我们的方法保留自我改进循环，并用
上界决定哪些候选值得付出这一代价。这改变了循环的经济性：生成一百个候选
很廉价，验证一百个候选却不然，因此筛选步骤成为决定方法预算的部分。

arc and bounds the cost of every order. Given these precedents, we claim
as new neither the upper bound itself (Proposition 1 in Section 4.2), which
follows from keeping the take-off and landing points of one solved order, nor
the idea of assigning a cost to an order without solving its SOCP. Our claim
concerns their use to train a DRL policy: as a reward for routes that are
never solved and as a ranking of candidate imitation labels, each of which is
verified exactly before it is used.

3.2. Cost structure 代价结构
    Two features of this formulation drive the method. First, constraints
(2)–(5) involve the variables of a single target and make no reference to
π. Second, the order appears only in (6), which links the landing point
of one target to the take-off point of the next. Any solution that fixes all
sortie variables therefore induces a closed-form cost for every order, since
the leg times are the only remaining quantities. Evaluating a fixed-order
problem with J targets takes tens of milliseconds at moderate sizes in our
implementation, about 0.03 s per call at J ≈ 60, while evaluating a table
sum takes microseconds. The number of orders that can be scored in one

                                             15
solve time differs by several orders of magnitude, which is the leverage the
method relies on. This is the structure that the next section exploits. The
policy in Section 4 selects π, and the SOCP determines the sortie points, so
the method follows the natural decomposition into a discrete upper level and
a continuous lower level. Only the upper level is learned.
  该模型的两个特征驱动了我们的方法。第一，约束 (2)–(5) 只涉及单个目标的
变量，与 π 无关。第二，顺序只出现在 (6) 中，它把一个目标的降落点与下一个目
标的起飞点联系起来。因此，任何固定了全部架次变量的解都为每个顺序诱导出一
个闭式代价，因为剩下的只有航段时间。在我们的实现中，求解一个有 J 个目标
的定序问题在中等规模下需要数十毫秒，J ≈ 60 时约 0.03 s 一次，而计算表求和
只需几微秒。在一次求解的时间内能打分的顺序数相差几个数量级，这正是该方
法依赖的杠杆。下一节利用的就是这一结构。第 4 节中的策略选择 π，SOCP 确定
架次点，因此方法遵循离散上层与连续下层的自然分解，只有上层是学习得到的。


4. Methodology 方法
4.1. Overview 概览
    Figure 1 shows one training step, and Table 3 lists it step by step for a
batch of instances. A frozen reference policy samples 200 candidate routes
per instance. A small edge predictor selects one of them as the anchor, and
the anchor is solved exactly. The solution yields a table C that scores every
candidate. Local search on the table produces an expert route, which is
verified exactly before it can serve as an imitation label. The learning policy
then receives two updates: a reinforcement step on rewards drawn mainly
from the table, and an imitation step on the verified experts. The number
of exact solves per instance is independent of the number of POMO routes,
which is what keeps the solver budget small. In POMO with an exact reward,
a batch of B instances with P routes costs BP solves. Here it costs at most
3B (one anchor, one policy route and at most one expert check per instance),
plus at most 2B in refresh rounds for instances with J ≥ 60, regardless of P ;
the training run averaged 2.89 solves per instance.

4.3. Policy, anchor selection and solver-free expert search 策略、锚点选择
      与无需求解器的专家搜索
    The learning policy is an attention encoder–decoder of the POMO type
(Kwon et al., 2020), with 4 encoder layers, embedding dimension 256, 16
attention heads and feed-forward dimension 512. Each node has six features:
its coordinates relative to the depot, divided by the largest depot–target dis-
tance of the instance; its distance to the depot on the same scale; a depot
indicator; the speed ratio vc /vd ; and the drone reach vd E on the same scale.
The speed and endurance features let one network serve instances with dif-
ferent operating parameters. The policy decodes one route per POMO start,
and in training every target serves as a start, so P = J.
  学习策略是 POMO 型的注意力编码器–解码器 (Kwon et al., 2020)，有 4 个编
码层，嵌入维度 256，16 个注意力头，前馈维度 512。每个节点有六个特征：相对
基地的坐标，除以该实例中基地到目标的最大距离；以同一尺度计的到基地距离；
基地标记；速度比 vc /vd ；以及以同一尺度计的无人机航程 vd E。速度和续航特征
使一个网络能够处理运行参数不同的实例。策略对每个 POMO 起点解码一条路
线；训练中每个目标都作为起点，所以 P = J。
   Two policies interact during training. The learning policy is updated
at every step. A frozen reference policy generates search material and is
replaced only when the learning policy obtains a strictly better exact mean
on a fixed validation set of 1,000 instances. This gate ensures that the expert
search starts from routes of validated quality.

                                      21
  训练中有两个策略相互作用。学习策略在每一步都更新。冻结的参考策略生成
搜索素材，只有当学习策略在一个固定的 1,000 个实例的验证集上取得严格更好
的精确均值时才被替换。这一门控保证专家搜索从质量经过验证的路线出发。
    An edge predictor maps the detached encoder output to a non-negative
directed table of size (J +1)×(J +1). It is trained with a Huber loss towards
tables built from exact solutions, and its loss does not reach the policy. Its
only role is to rank the 200 reference routes and choose the anchor, so a poor
prediction costs search quality and cannot affect validity.
  边预测器把切断梯度后的编码器输出映射为一张大小为 (J + 1) × (J + 1) 的非
负有向表。它以 Huber 损失向由精确解构建的表回归，其损失不会传到策略。它
唯一的作用是对 200 条参考路线排序并选出锚点，因此预测不准只会降低搜索质
量，不会影响有效性。
    The anchor table scores all sampled routes. We select 30 starts: the 20
routes with the lowest bound, and 10 further routes chosen for diversity from
a pool of the 60 next-best routes within a relative gap of 5%. From each
start, directed 2-opt runs on the table for at most 30 moves and accepts only
strict decreases of Uq . Local search on the table is complementary to LKH
(Lin and Kernighan, 1973; Helsgaun, 2000): on large instances, a share of the
starts is also passed to LKH as an asymmetric TSP on C q . In every case the
search minimises a surrogate that can only overestimate the true cost. The
LKH time budget for all participating starts is deterministic, 1.5 × 10−4 J 2

predicted in advance.
    锚点表为所有采样路线打分。我们选出 30 个起点：上界最低的 20 条路线，以

起点出发，有向 2-opt 在表上最多运行 30 步，只接受 Uq 的严格下降。表上的局
部搜索与 LKH (Lin and Kernighan, 1973; Helsgaun, 2000) 互补：在大实例上，部
分起点还会作为 C q 上的非对称 TSP 交给 LKH。无论哪种情况，搜索最小化的都

provement. Its exact cost is then computed once, and the route becomes
the expert. If no route clears the threshold, the anchor remains the expert.

With a small probability, the near-miss rule therefore solves the route with
the smallest relative gap above the threshold and accepts it if it is exactly

better. The probability adapts to training progress: for small instances it
moves between 1% and 5% as the imitation accuracy and the share of policy
routes that beat the expert rise. Each instance has a budget of one such

the exact cost of a near-miss route exceeds its bound, the run stops, because
that would indicate a numerical or implementation fault.
    命题 1 给出筛选规则。若一条路线满足 Uq (π) < f (q)，则无需求解即有

every accepted label is exact, whichever rule proposed it.

且真正最好的路线在 8% 和 12% 的实例中得到了负优势。由于上界在锚点附近最
紧，这种偏差也可能偏向与锚点相似的路线；第 6.3 节将回到这一点。
    A separate optimiser step performs teacher-forced cross-entropy on the
expert sequences. Each batch mixes current experts with a replay buffer of
50,000 experts, which supplies 30% of the batch. Only verified experts enter
this update, so an error in the bound can waste a solve but can never become
a label. Imitation runs for five inner epochs at each update on batches of
1,000 sequences, split into micro-batches of 128. The two updates use a
common network and take separate optimiser steps.
   单独的一个优化器步骤对专家序列做教师强制的交叉熵训练。每批将当前专家
与一个容量为 50,000 的专家回放缓冲区混合，后者占批次的 30%。只有经过验证
的专家进入这一更新，因此上界的误差最多浪费一次求解，永远不会变成标签。每
次更新时，模仿学习在 1,000 条序列的批上运行五个内部轮次，并拆分为大小为
128 的微批。两种更新使用同一个网络，分别执行各自的优化器步骤。

4.5. Progressive search 渐进式搜索
    Search effort is scheduled by instance size and training stage. Instances
with J < 60 keep the plain 2-opt search throughout. For J ≥ 60, a ramp r =
clip((e − 40)/60, 0, 1) at epoch e raises the fraction of LKH starts to r/2 and
raises the near-miss probability towards 1. After epoch 60, large instances
also receive two refresh rounds: the most recently solved route supplies a
new table, which is tight around that route, and the search continues on it.
Each round solves at most one additional route.
  搜索力度按实例规模和训练阶段调度。J < 60 的实例始终使用普通 2-opt 搜
索。对于 J ≥ 60，第 e 轮的斜坡 r = clip((e − 40)/60, 0, 1) 把 LKH 起点比例提
高到 r/2，并把近失概率提高到接近 1。第 60 轮之后，大实例还会进行两轮刷新：
最近求解的路线提供一张新表，它在该路线附近是紧的，搜索在其上继续。每轮最
多额外求解一条路线。
   The quantity of imitation labels is normalised at the same time. An im-
proved expert is repeated in the imitation batch so that the expected number

                                      25
of samples per instance stays near 1.44, with the repeat weight clipped to
[1, 5]. Without this step, the imitation pressure would grow as the search
improves.
  与此同时，模仿标签的数量被归一化。改进后的专家在模仿批次中被重复，使
每个实例的期望样本数保持在 1.44 左右，重复权重裁剪到 [1, 5]。没有这一步，模
仿压力会随搜索改进而增长。
   The operator mix and size threshold were chosen in earlier development
runs. The final recipe uses 2-opt and LKH without swap. The ramp over
epochs 40 to 100 was chosen so that the policy first learns from the cheapest
search and receives the stronger expert only once it has stabilised. We did
not run a formal search over these values, and Section 6 returns to this point.
  算子组合和规模阈值在早期开发运行中选定。最终方案使用 2-opt 和 LKH，不
使用 swap。第 40 至 100 轮的斜坡是这样选择的：策略先从最廉价的搜索中学习，
在稳定之后才接受更强的专家。我们没有对这些取值做正式搜索，第 6 节会回到

5.7. Ablation study 消融实验
    The ablations measure what individual components contribute at a fixed
point in training; they report component effects and are not offered as a
decomposition of the saving. Variants B and D train from scratch to one
million instances (epoch 100), with the configuration and seed of the pro-
posed method otherwise unchanged. They are compared with Group C, the
proposed method at the same point, on the same 500 test instances with
eight views, by size. We fixed the decision rule before seeing the results: an
effect is reported only if the paired per-instance relative cost gap between
the variant and C has a 95% interval that excludes zero. The intervals are
pointwise and are not adjusted for comparisons across sizes. Conclusions
apply to epoch 100, before the reported checkpoint E133, and each variant is
a single run. The comparison fixes training instances and epochs, not solver
calls or wall time.

6. Discussion and implications for practice 讨论与实践启示
6.1. When the approach is worthwhile 何时值得采用该方法
    A trained policy moves most of the computing effort into training, but
not all of it: at test time E133 still solves one SOCP per distinct decoded
route, about 10.3 s of single-core time per Real529 instance, or 0.78 s of wall
time with 16 solver workers. Within the range of speeds and endurance seen
in training, the policy can be reused without retraining, because both enter
as input features. The same holds for G200, which gives better plans at
the same test time. The smaller training budget therefore matters when a
learned solver has to be trained from scratch, for example for a variant with
additional constraints or a different spatial distribution of targets. In that
situation the solver budget of training decides whether a learned solver is
affordable at all, and our results show that a useful policy can be obtained
with a few million solves instead of hundreds of millions. We did not test
training on a changed problem.
  训练好的策略把大部分计算工作移到训练阶段，但并非全部：在测试时，E133
仍要为每条不同的解码路线求解一个 SOCP，每个 Real529 实例约需 10.3 s 单核
时间，或在 16 个求解进程下 0.78 s 墙钟时间。在训练中见过的速度和续航范围内，
策略无需重新训练即可复用，因为两者都作为输入特征。G200 同样如此，并且在
相同测试时间下给出更好的方案。因此，更小的训练预算只在必须从头训练学习
型求解器时才有意义，例如面对增加了约束或目标空间分布不同的变体。在这种
情况下，训练的求解器预算决定了学习型求解器是否负担得起；我们的结果表明，

6.2. What the loose bound does and does not show 宽松上界说明了什么、
     没有说明什么
    The bound is far from tight on routes that differ from the anchor. The
lowest bound among policy routes exceeds the exact cost of that route by
33.6% at epoch 133 (Table 10), and near-miss routes, which come out of local
search, carry bounds 14.6% above the anchor cost on average although 40.8%
of them are exactly better (Section 4.3). The bound is thus not accurate
near the anchor at the resolution that matters for acceptance, and most late-
stage experts are accepted through near-miss checks and refresh rounds. In
the recipe it serves mainly as a reward for routes that are never solved and
as a ranking that decides which few routes receive an exact solve, while the
exactness of every label keeps errors in the bound out of imitation learning.
  对于与锚点不同的路线，上界远不紧。第 133 轮时，策略路线中的最低上界比
该路线的精确代价高 33.6%（表 10） ，而来自局部搜索的近失路线，其上界平均比
锚点代价高 14.6%，尽管其中 40.8% 精确地更好（第 4.3 节）
                                    。因此，在对接受与
否起决定作用的精度上，上界在锚点附近并不准确，后期大多数专家是通过近失
检验和刷新轮次被接受的。在训练方案中，它主要作为从未求解路线的奖励，以及
决定哪几条路线获得精确求解的排序，而每个标签的精确性使上界的误差不会进
入模仿学习。
    Two experiments bear on whether a tighter or less biased signal would
help, and neither settles the question. In a 30-epoch continuation from E133,
taking the minimum over all tables available for each route used about two ad-
ditional tables per instance on the affected batches and tightened the bound
by only 0.32%. This is a small change against an excess of about one third,
so it does not show that tightness has low value. In an earlier 20-epoch
study from scratch, rewards from exact solves of a random subset of 4 to 8
routes per instance point in a direction much closer to the all-exact advantage
(an expected cosine of about 0.85 to 0.95, against about 0.6 for the hybrid


                                      44
reward). They cost three to four times the Gurobi CPU. These are training-
signal diagnostics from short single-seed runs, not eight-view policy-quality
comparisons. The hybrid reward is also biased, not only noisy (Section 4.4),
and whether a tighter or unbiased signal would close the remaining gap is
open. Two refinements that keep the guarantee are available and untested:
letting each target take its sortie from a different solved solution, chosen by
a dynamic programme along the route, and letting each target exchange its
take-off and landing points, chosen by a two-state dynamic programme along
the route.
   有两项实验与更紧或偏差更小的信号是否有帮助相关，但都不能定论。在从
E133 出发的 30 轮续训中，对每条路线取所有可用表上的最小值，在受影响的批次
上每个实例约多用两张表，只把上界收紧了 0.32%。相对于约三分之一的超出量，
这是很小的变化，因此不能说明紧度的价值低。在一项更早的、从头开始的 20 轮
研究中，对每个实例随机 4 至 8 条路线做精确求解得到的奖励，其方向与全精确
优势接近得多（期望余弦约 0.85 至 0.95，而混合奖励约为 0.6）。它们的 Gurobi
CPU 成本为三到四倍。这些是短期单种子运行的训练信号诊断，不是八视角策略
解质量比较。混合奖励还有偏差，而不仅是有噪声（第 4.4 节），更紧或无偏的信
号能否消除剩余差距仍是开放问题。有两种保持保证的改进可用但尚未测试：让
每个目标从不同的已求解方案中取架次（沿路线用动态规划选择），以及让每个目
标互换其起飞点和降落点（沿路线用两状态动态规划选择）。

6.3. Limitations 局限性
    Six limitations apply. First, E133 remains behind G200 on synthetic
instances from the training distribution, by +1.13% with eight views. We
have not identified the cause, and several explanations remain open. (i)
Route diversity: in a diagnostic on a later checkpoint of the same run (epoch
163), the edge overlap between POMO routes was higher than for G200 at
every size tested (0.64, 0.67 and 0.62 against 0.48, 0.55 and 0.53 at J = 20,
60 and 100). Against this explanation, the overlap difference shrinks with
size while the gap grows. (ii) Pull towards the anchor: the bound is tightest
at the anchor and the hybrid reward favours the solved route (Section 4.4),
so the reward may draw the policy towards anchor-like routes. In matched
20-epoch runs from the same initialisation, replacing the bound-based reward
with random-subset exact rewards lowered the overlap at J = 20 from 0.374
to 0.330 and from 0.379 to 0.307. (iii) Training length and recipe: E133 saw
15 times fewer instances and uses a different network, and G200 trained with
J/4 starts. Continuations from E133 that changed the bound or the imitation
weight did not move the overlap appreciably, so they do not discriminate

                                      45
between (i) and (ii). Separating these explanations requires controlled runs
that we have not performed. Second, every model is one training run with
one seed, and the reported intervals cover variation over test instances only,
not over training runs. Large differences, such as the 3.8% gap at equal solver
budget on Real529, are unlikely to reverse with another seed, but small ones,
such as the +0.21% gap on Real529, should be read as closeness and not as
a ranking. Third, Real529 is not held out and covers only two parameter
settings, both at the edges of the training range. Fourth, the comparison
on time depends on hardware and implementation, which is why we treat
solver calls as the primary measure. Fifth, the settings of the progressive
search were set by development runs and not by a formal search, so the
reported performance may not be the best attainable for the method. Sixth,
the bound depends on the separable structure of the CVTSP. It extends to
a variant only if sortie constraints remain independent of the order, which
should hold for several sorties of fixed composition but fails if, for instance,
target time windows couple the sorties.
    有六项局限。第一，E133 在训练分布的合成实例上仍落后于 G200，八视角下
为 +1.13%。我们尚未确定原因，有几种解释仍然开放。(i) 路线多样性：在同一
运行较晚检查点（第 163 轮）的诊断中，POMO 路线之间的边重合度在所有测试
规模上都高于 G200（J = 20、60、100 时为 0.64、0.67、0.62，对比 0.48、0.55、
0.53）。反对这一解释的证据是：重合度差异随规模缩小，而差距随规模增大。(ii)
向锚点的牵引：上界在锚点处最紧，混合奖励偏向被求解的路线（第 4.4 节），因
此奖励可能把策略拉向与锚点相似的路线。在从相同初始化出发、相互匹配的 20
轮运行中，把基于上界的奖励换成随机子集精确奖励，使 J = 20 时的重合度从
0.374 降到 0.330、从 0.379 降到 0.307。(iii) 训练长度与方案：E133 见过的实例
少 15 倍、使用不同的网络，而 G200 以 J/4 个起点训练。从 E133 出发改变上界
或模仿权重的续训，没有明显改变重合度，因此它们无法区分 (i) 和 (ii)。要区分
这些解释，需要我们尚未进行的受控运行。第二，每个模型都只是一次单种子的训
练运行，报告的区间只覆盖测试实例上的变异，不覆盖训练运行之间的变异。较
大的差异（如 Real529 上等求解预算下 3.8% 的差距）不太可能因换种子而逆转，
但较小的差异（如 Real529 上 +0.21% 的差距）应理解为接近，而不是排名。第
三，Real529 不是留出集，且只覆盖两种参数设置，都位于训练范围的边缘。第四，
时间比较依赖硬件和实现，这就是我们把求解器调用作为主要度量的原因。第五，
渐进式搜索的设置来自开发运行，而不是正式搜索，因此报告的性能未必是该方
法能达到的最好水平。第六，上界依赖 CVTSP 的可分结构。只有当架次约束仍与
顺序无关时，它才能推广到一个变体；对于组成固定的多个架次应当成立，但如果
例如目标时间窗把各架次耦合起来，则不成立。




                                      46
7. Conclusion 结论
    This paper asked whether a DRL policy for the CVTSP needs an ex-
act solver reward for every route. One optimal fixed-order solution yields
an upper bound on all visiting orders, computed by summing table entries,
and a training recipe built on this bound, with 2.89 exact solves per train-
ing instance and exact verification of every imitation label, obtains a useful
policy at a small fraction of the usual solver budget. With the same 3.84
million solves, POMO trained on exact rewards is on average 3.8% worse per
instance than the policy from our recipe on the literature benchmark. The
fully trained baseline used 78 times more solves in training, or about 29 times
more when both runs are counted as run, and it remains better: by +0.21%
on the literature benchmark and +0.72% on larger instances, where the two
are close, and by +1.13% with augmentation on synthetic instances from
the training distribution. Iterated local search and variable neighbourhood
search given ten times the single-core time of the policy obtain better solu-
tions than E133, most clearly at 100 and 200 targets. The recipe is therefore
a way to obtain a learned CVTSP solver with a small solver budget, not a
better solver.
   本文研究的问题是：CVTSP 的 DRL 策略是否需要为每条路线提供精确求解
器奖励。一个最优定序解给出所有访问顺序的上界，可通过对表元素求和得到；基
于这一上界构建的训练方案，每个训练实例使用 2.89 次精确求解，并对每个模仿
标签做精确验证，只用通常求解器预算的一小部分就得到了一个有用的策略。在
同样 384 万次求解下，以精确奖励训练的 POMO 在文献基准上逐实例平均比本
方案的策略差 3.8%。完全训练的基线在训练中多用了 78 倍的求解，若两边都按
实际运行计则约为 29 倍，并且仍然更好：本方案策略与它的差距在文献基准上为
+0.21%，在更大实例上为 +0.72%，二者在这些集合上接近；在训练分布的合成实
例上，增强后为 +1.13%。给予策略十倍单核时间的迭代局部搜索和变邻域搜索，
能得到比 E133 更好的解，在 100 和 200 个目标时最为明显。因此，本方案是以
较小求解器预算获得学习型 CVTSP 求解器的方法，而不是一个更好的求解器。
    Several directions follow. The recipe should be tested where training
from scratch is needed, such as variants with further constraints or other
target distributions. The training signal can be tightened while keeping its
guarantee, through the per-target combination of tables and the reversal
symmetry. The explanations for the remaining gap, route diversity, the pull
of a biased reward towards the anchor and the difference in training length,
need to be separated by controlled runs. The table could also support search
at test time or warm starts for metaheuristics. The bound requires only that

the sorties of one solution remain feasible for other orders, but whether it is
useful elsewhere also depends on how loose it is, which we could not predict
in advance. Several carriers (Fahradyan et al., 2020) and several UAVs on
one vessel (Ren et al., 2027) raise different questions, since sorties may then
overlap in time and the leg structure changes.
   由此引出几个方向。应在需要从头训练的场景中测试该方案，例如有更多约束
或其他目标分布的变体。可以在保持保证的前提下收紧训练信号，方法包括多张
表的逐目标组合以及反转对称性。剩余差距的几种解释，即路线多样性、有偏奖励
向锚点的牵引以及训练长度的差异，需要通过受控运行加以区分。表还可以支持
测试时的搜索，或为元启发式提供热启动。上界只要求一个解的架次对其他顺序
仍然可行，但它在其他问题上是否有用还取决于它有多宽松，这一点我们无法事
先预测。多个载体 (Fahradyan et al., 2020) 和一艘船上的多架无人机 (Ren et al.,
2027) 会带来不同的问题，因为此时架次可能在时间上重叠，航段结构也会改变。
