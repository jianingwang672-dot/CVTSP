## Acceptance Criteria Paraphrase

D1 section_completeness: every section of the approved outline will be written, and any unresolved gap will be visible, not hidden.

D2 citation_density: every factual claim will carry a citation or be supported by this paper's own data. Absence claims will state the search bounds and name the nearest prior work.

D3 argument_blueprint_fidelity: each section will follow the claims, evidence and reasoning assigned in the blueprint.

D4 total_word_count: the total will stay within ten percent of the declared target.

D5 per_section_word_count: each section will stay within fifteen percent of its allocation.

D6 paragraph_structure: each paragraph will make one clear point with support and a link to the section purpose, and sections will connect to each other.

D7 register_consistency: the text will hold a formal operations research register throughout.
